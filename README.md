### Hi, I'm Amir

Electrical engineering student at San José State University, interested in hardware
debugging, embedded systems, and the point where hardware meets software.

**What I work on**

- **Homelab:** a two-node Proxmox VE cluster running about a dozen services in LXC
  containers and Docker. One RTX 3080 Ti handles hardware video transcoding and local
  LLM inference at the same time.
- **Remote PC power button:** a XIAO ESP32-C3 drives an optocoupler wired to my PC's
  front-panel power switch, mounted on a 3D-printed PCIe bracket and controlled through
  Home Assistant.
- **In progress, wearable now-playing display:** a battery-powered bag charm that shows
  the song playing on my phone, using a XIAO ESP32-C3 driving a 256x64 SSD1322 OLED
  over SPI, with track info sent over Bluetooth LE.
- **Embedded:** recovered a bricked Meshtastic LoRa node (RAK4631, nRF52840) by updating
  its UF2 bootloader and reflashing firmware over USB.
- **Repair:** board-level troubleshooting with a multimeter, from no-boot desktops to
  headphone rewiring.

**Tools**

Linux, Proxmox, Docker, ESP32, nRF52840, SPI, Bluetooth LE, C (learning), soldering,
oscilloscope, digital multimeter

**Currently**

Learning C in my microcontrollers course and building whatever I’m curious about next.
